How Multi-Company Quotations Work

When you build a quotation, the system automatically produces 3 separate quotations: one for Company 1 at base price, one for Company 2 with a +2% price adjustment, and one for Company 3 with a +3% price adjustment. Each estimate renders the respective company's official name, logo, NTN/STRN, and contact info on its PDF.
