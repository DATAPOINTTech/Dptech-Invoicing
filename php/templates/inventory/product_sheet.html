How to Connect your Google Sheet

1
Ensure Sheet has correct columns:

Your Google Sheet can have columns: Product Name, SKU, Category, Current Rate (Purchase Price), Unit, Description.

2
Set Sharing to "Anyone with the link can view":

In Google Sheets, click the blue "Share" button at top right. Under "General access", select "Anyone with the link" as "Viewer", then click "Copy link".

3
Paste and Click "Sync Now":

Paste the link into the URL field above and hit Sync Now. The app will pull all products and rates, mark them as purchase price, and automatically apply +10% profit in quotations!
